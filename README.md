# Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive overview of business performance across products, regions, sales channels, and time.

The dashboard helps analyze key sales KPIs, identify high-performing products and regions, and understand revenue trends over time.

---

1.Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to build an interactive dashboard that provides a clear view of sales and financial performance.

The dashboard enables users to:

- Monitor total revenue and profit
- Track total orders and units sold
- Analyze profit margin
- Understand monthly revenue trends
- Compare revenue across products
- Analyze regional performance
- Compare different sales channels
- Identify top-performing products
- Filter results by category, region, sales channel, and order date

3. Dashboard Components

# 1. Monthly Revenue Trend

A monthly column chart showing revenue performance throughout the year.

This visualization helps identify:

- Revenue growth and decline
- Highest-performing months
- Seasonal patterns
- Monthly fluctuations

---

# 2. Revenue by Products

A horizontal bar chart comparing revenue generated by different products.

Products included:

- Laptop
- Smartphone
- Tablet
- Monitor
- Printer
- Headphones

This makes it easy to identify the products contributing the most revenue.

---

# 3. Revenue by Region

A donut chart showing revenue distribution across different regions:

- East
- South
- West
- North

This helps compare geographical sales performance.

---

# 4. Revenue by Sales Channel

A column chart comparing revenue generated through:

- Online
- Retail Store
- Distributor

This provides insight into which sales channel contributes most to overall revenue.

---

# 5. Top 5 Products by Revenue

A focused view of the five products generating the highest revenue.

This helps management quickly identify the strongest products in the portfolio.
